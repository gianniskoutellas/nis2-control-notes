# NIS2 Control Notes

Practical mapping of selected NIS2 obligations to control questions and evidence examples.

This repository supports junior GRC / compliance work by turning directive-level requirements into concrete questions an analyst can ask and evidence that can be requested.

**Scope:** Selected obligations relevant to essential and important entities under the NIS2 Directive. Not legal advice. Not a complete implementation guide.

**Author context:** Built alongside an MSc thesis on EU cybersecurity strategy (NIS2, Cyber Resilience Act, ENISA mandate).

---

## How to use this repo

1. Start with the overview table below.
2. Open individual obligation notes for control questions and example evidence.
3. Adapt questions to the organisation’s size, sector, and existing controls.

## Structure of each note

- **Obligation (plain language)**
- **Control questions** an analyst can ask
- **Example evidence** that could demonstrate the control
- **Common gaps** seen in early-stage programmes

## Disclaimer

These notes are educational and illustrative. They do not replace official guidance, legal counsel, or organisation-specific risk decisions. Always refer to the official NIS2 text, national transposition, and ENISA / national authority guidance for formal compliance work.
