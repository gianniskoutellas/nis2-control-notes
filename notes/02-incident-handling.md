# 02 — Incident Handling

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must have capabilities to prevent, detect, respond to and recover from incidents, and to report significant incidents to the competent authority / CSIRT within required timelines.

## Control questions

- Is there a documented incident response plan (IRP) with roles and escalation paths?
- How are security events detected and triaged?
- What is the process for classifying an incident as “significant” under national rules?
- Who is responsible for notifying the competent authority / CSIRT?
- Are playbooks tested (tabletop or technical exercises)?
- How is post-incident learning captured and fed back into controls?

## Example evidence

- Incident response plan and contact lists
- Detection and escalation procedures (or SOC runbooks)
- Incident logs / ticket examples (redacted)
- Records of notifications to authorities (if any)
- Exercise / tabletop reports and lessons learned
- Evidence of updates made after incidents or exercises

## Common gaps

- Plan exists but staff do not know it
- No clear criteria for “significant” incident
- Detection relies only on end-user reports
- No post-incident review process
