# 04 — Supply Chain Security

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must address security-related aspects concerning the relationships with their direct suppliers and service providers, including the overall quality and resilience of products and services.

## Control questions

- Is there an inventory of critical suppliers and ICT service providers?
- How is cyber risk assessed for new and existing suppliers?
- Are security requirements included in contracts (e.g. incident notification, access control, audit rights)?
- How are supplier incidents that affect the entity detected and handled?
- Is there a process for periodic review of high-risk suppliers?

## Example evidence

- Critical supplier / third-party register
- Supplier risk assessment questionnaire or methodology
- Contract clauses addressing security and incident reporting
- Records of supplier security reviews or SOC reports received
- Incident cases involving third parties (redacted)

## Common gaps

- No prioritisation of suppliers by criticality
- Security requirements only in policy, not in contracts
- No ongoing monitoring after onboarding
