# 01 — Risk Management

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must take appropriate and proportionate technical, operational and organisational measures to manage the risks posed to the security of network and information systems. Measures should be based on an all-hazards approach and be proportionate to risk.

## Control questions

- Is there a documented information security / cyber risk policy approved by management?
- When was the last organisation-wide or system-level risk assessment performed?
- How are risks identified, scored, and prioritised?
- Who owns residual risk acceptance?
- Are risk treatment plans tracked to completion?
- How often is the risk register reviewed?

## Example evidence

- Approved information security / cyber risk policy
- Risk assessment methodology and latest assessment report
- Risk register with owners, ratings, and treatment status
- Management review or committee minutes referencing cyber risk
- Evidence of residual risk acceptance decisions

## Common gaps

- Policy exists but is not operationalised
- Risk assessment is one-off and outdated
- No clear link between risks and funded treatment actions
- Residual risk accepted informally with no record
