# 07 — Cyber Hygiene and Training

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must implement basic cyber hygiene practices and cybersecurity training for staff, so that people understand risks and their role in reducing them.

## Control questions

- Is there mandatory security awareness training for employees?
- How often is training refreshed?
- Are higher-risk roles (admins, developers, finance) given additional training?
- Are basic hygiene measures in place (e.g. patching policy, strong authentication, least privilege)?
- How is phishing or social engineering risk addressed?

## Example evidence

- Awareness training policy and completion records
- Training materials or platform reports
- Role-based training for privileged users
- Policies on passwords / MFA, patching, acceptable use
- Phishing simulation results (if used) and follow-up actions

## Common gaps

- One-time induction training only
- No tracking of completion
- Privileged users treated the same as general staff
