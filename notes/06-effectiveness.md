# 06 — Effectiveness of Measures

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must implement measures to assess the effectiveness of their cybersecurity risk-management measures (e.g. through testing, audits, or reviews).

## Control questions

- How does the organisation know its controls are working?
- Are there internal or external audits of cyber / information security controls?
- Are technical tests performed (vulnerability scans, penetration tests, configuration reviews)?
- Are metrics or KPIs defined for security performance?
- How are findings tracked to remediation?

## Example evidence

- Audit plans and recent audit reports (internal or external)
- Vulnerability scan / penetration test executive summaries and remediation trackers
- Security metrics dashboards or periodic reports to management
- Evidence of corrective actions closed

## Common gaps

- Controls implemented but never tested
- Findings sit open with no owner or deadline
- Metrics exist but are not reviewed by decision-makers
