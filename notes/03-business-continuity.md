# 03 — Business Continuity

## Obligation (plain language)

Entities must ensure business continuity, including backup management, disaster recovery, and crisis management, so that critical functions can continue or be restored after disruption.

## Control questions

- Which services / systems are identified as critical?
- Are recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) defined?
- How often are backups taken, tested, and protected from ransomware?
- Is there a documented disaster recovery / business continuity plan?
- When was the last restore or continuity test performed?
- Who activates crisis management and how is communication handled?

## Example evidence

- Business impact analysis or criticality classification
- Backup policy and recent successful restore test results
- Disaster recovery / BCP document with roles
- Test reports (tabletop or technical)
- Crisis communication plan and contact trees

## Common gaps

- Backups exist but restores are never tested
- Critical systems not clearly prioritised
- Plans outdated or owned by a single person
- No offline / immutable backup consideration
